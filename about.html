About us

Anagram Panda is a simple word game where every round is a new challenge.

Build words from the letters on the board, find longer and more valuable words, and try to beat your personal best.

The game is designed to be quick to learn, easy to play, and challenging to master.

How to Play

You have a limited amount of time to create words from the available letters.

Choose your letters, build a valid word, and submit it. Longer words are worth more points.

Your highest score is based on your best single word.
